# Code of Conduct

Pawtrol exists to help neighbors look out for each other. We expect the same in how we work together.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many contributors are new to open source, React Native or Postgres.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Disagree openly and politely, and accept the maintainers' call once a decision is made.
- Respect people's privacy: never share someone's personal information, including anything you come across in reports, logs or test data.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Omargn/pawtrol/security/advisories/new), marking it as a conduct report, so it isn't public. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn or ban anyone who does so, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and repeated it is.
